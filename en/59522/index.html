hihi stranger, thanks for stopping by and checking out my space ヾ(•ω•`)o

Hi There~ I’m Lave

My full handle is Sacilave, but you can just call me Lave.

I have a huge soft spot for cute things. My dream is to become a multidisciplinary creator. (〃￣︶￣)人(￣︶￣〃)

Wherever you are in the world, and by whatever serendipity you stumbled across this little corner of the web, since you’re here, it must be fate. If you feel like we’d vibe, dear stranger, don’t hesitate to reach out—feel free to leave a comment or connect with me on social media!

If you ever need a listening ear, a bit of emotional support, or just want someone to share your story with, you’re always welcome! (。・ω・。)

Maybe one day I’ll launch my own channel to make content. My ultimate dream is to build up a solid nest egg and travel all over the world.

My Profile ┏ (゜ω゜)=👉

Tech

  • Mainly focused on Unity game development, Apps, and Web. I’ve also tinkered with Mini Programs, Raspberry Pi, microcontrollers, and various other tech stacks.
  • My go-to languages are mainly C# | Java | Python | C/C++ | JS | … My default state of being is just constantly tinkering with random projects.

ACG

  • Anime (Dark/Depressing, Wholesome/Healing, Romance, Plot-heavy)
    All-time favorites: Steins;Gate, The Melancholy of Haruhi Suzumiya, Death Note, Girls’ Last Tour, Made in Abyss, Demon Slayer: Kimetsu no Yaiba, Takopi’s Original Sin, Attack on Titan…
    I’ve watched quite a lot of anime, dating back to Vampire Hunter D, A Certain Magical Index, Samurai Champloo… Though nowadays seasonal anime feels pretty dry, so I rarely watch new shows.
    The gateway anime that started it all: Akame ga Kill!
  • Galgame / Visual Novels (Dark/Depressing, Wholesome/Healing, Romance, Plot-heavy)
    Favorites: Saya no Uta (Song of Saya), Summer Pockets, *Eden, The Magician on Paper (Kami no Ue no Mahoutsukai), Doki Doki Literature Club…

Photography

  • Feel free to check out my Gallery
  • Honestly, I barely have time for street photography these days, so there aren’t that many photos.

Gaming

  • I love RPGs, multiplayer games, and emotionally gripping, story-driven titles. PC is my primary platform, but I also play on Switch, 3DS, DS, PS Vita, and PSP.
    (No PlayStation home consoles because I don’t own a PS5, just handhelds like the PSP and PS Vita). I rarely touch mobile games, except for the occasional round of Uno (￣y▽ ￣)╭
  • Favorites: The Witcher 3, Monster Hunter, Assassin’s Creed series (Assassin’s Creed II was my gateway PC game back in elementary school), OneShot, Kirby series, Stardew Valley, Elden Ring, Yakuza / Like a Dragon series, Minecraft…
    I won’t list them all out, since there are just way too many (´▽｀)
  • I also play FPS and competitive games—my most played is R6S (Rainbow Six Siege, though I’ve pretty much hung up my coat), and lately I’ve been playing Valorant (CN Server).
  • … For more details, feel free to check out my Steam Page.

Music

  1. Absolute favorite: ZUTOMAYO
  2. Kenshi Yonezu, Fujii Kaze, Vaundy, XXXTENTACION, YOASOBI, Rokudenashi
  3. LUCA, Masaki Suda, Billie Eilish, mizuki, TK from Ling Tosite Sigure, King Gnu, Yuuri, Sayuri, Aimer, Ado
  • Sora Amamiya, Kano, KAF, MINNIE, Reol, rionos (Pocket wo Fukuramasete—I truly love this track), Sana, Eve, Minami
  • tuki., Haruka Nakamura, Rokudenashi, DAZBEE, imase, GReeeeN, Soutaiseiriron, Lovely Summer Chan (Suisei—absolutely adore this one), RIM, Toiki
  • Natsunose, Mirei Toyama, Daoko, JVKE, Ari Abdul, DECO*27, milet, KAFU, Inabakumori, R3HAB (used to be a big fan of this DJ Mag Top 100 artist), Vicetone
